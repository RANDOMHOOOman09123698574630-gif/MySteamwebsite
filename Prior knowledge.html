    Prior Knowledge

    Water purification can be done in many different ways including using chemicals or by doing filtration. By filtering water, we can remove microorganisms like bacteria. Using chemicals like iodine, chlorine is good for emergency water purification. Iodine and chlorine purify water by removing microorganisms. How effective it is depends on the concentration of the chemical. However, adding too many chemicals may be dangerous because from chlorine solutions, you may get burns and also it may leave too much of the chemical to drink or the water. So, to make water safe to drink, adding chemicals with not a high concentration and not so much in amount is the best. In addition to the chemical methods, activated carbon can also work while purifying water. Activated carbon works through absorption, where impurities and some chemicals in the water stick to the carbon. However, this process can not kill microorganisms, but removes odor and color from the water. Also distillation can help leave impurities behind after water evaporates. Filtration removes solids from the water. TDS Meters are devices that can check the purity of water. They measure in parts per million(ppm), and depending on the number, it can be suitable for different things. For example, 0-50 ppm is ideal water from deionization and microfiltration, and 500+ would be the highest value of contamination.
