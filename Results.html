    Conclusions

    In this experiment, we tested the methods of purifying water. Our hypothesis was that filtration methods and chemical methods combined would be much more affective than just one. However, the results showed how distillation is the best method of purifying. We found out that that when distillation method was the best as the ppm was very low and the ph was very close to 7. The filteration did not significantly lower the total dissolved solids ppm. However it somewhat lowered the ph level. Iodine only lowered the ph and made the ppm higher. This was because the iodine dissolved in the water. Chlorine was similar because the ppm became higher. However the Chlorine did not lower the ph. The chlorine+filtration, iodine+filtration made the lowered both ph and ppm.

      In short

    • •We learned that among the filtration methods, distilation is most affective as it made the dirty pond water nearly pure water
    • •We also learned that activated carbon is best among the chemical filtration methods for the ph. There was not any methods that lowered the ppm for chemicals.
    • •In the end, our hypothesis was correct, but it wasn’t as a big margin as we thought it woud be. for the ppm, activated carbon and distilation was the lowest.
