    What Method Would Filter Water The Best

      Abstract

      Our steam project is about purifying water with many methods and our research question is ‘What type of methods and chemicals can purify the dirty pond water the best?’ When we started, our hypothesis was that filtration methods and chemicals will purify the water the best because chemicals can remove microorganisms and filtration can remove particles in the water. We made dependent and independent variables. First, we got equipment like pond water, iodine, chlorine and the TDS meters to measure the ppm. We started by filtration. After we used chemicals then chemicals and filtration together. After that we re-did the project for try 2. After we did the second try we started doing all the distillation tries. We finished the experiment after 2 weeks of experements. We saw that most of the things only decreased the ph but the distillation highly decreased both ph and ppm. In conclusion, we found out that distillation was the most sucsessful!

        Hypotheses

        If water is purified using chemicals, the water will purify the water better than using filtration because filtration can not remove small particles from the water but chemical treatment can purify microorganisms. But using both will be the best because filtration methods help remove big particles while chemicals can purify microorganisms.

          Variables

          We made our and dependent variables Independent variables and dependent variables: Methods for purifying water, type of chemicals used Controlled variables: amount of water, amount of chemical used, time we wait until we measure.
